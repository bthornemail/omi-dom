Agreement is the closest thing to truth.

Two peers. No server, no clock, no authority, no merge function. One XOR says how far apart they are and exactly where. One more XOR puts them back together — because the distance and the displacement are the same object.

what this is, precisely

It is: detection and repair of divergence with no coordination.

It is not: a clock. XOR is commutative, so a ^ b == b ^ a — it measures the magnitude of a difference and never the order of two concurrent edits. It does not replace vector clocks and cannot tell you which edit came first. The narrow claim is the true one: clocks exist to order events, and if all you need is how far apart two states are and how to undo the difference, you need no clock at all.

Also: a ^ a == 0 for any a, anywhere, in any base. That is a logical truth, not a measurement, which is why it is the only thing here that is genuinely invariant.
